Our Story

Why I started Spotless Rides

“If I wouldn't hand it back to my own mother, it isn't done yet.”

— Founder, Spotless Rides

It started with a car nobody else wanted to touch.

My first car was a hand-me-down with faded paint, a coffee stain older than my driver's license, and headlights so cloudy they barely lit the road. I couldn't afford a shop detail, so I bought a cheap bucket, a bag of microfiber towels, and taught myself in the driveway — one YouTube video and one mistake at a time.

The first time I finished it properly, I sat in that car for ten minutes and just looked at it. Same car. Same engine. Same miles. It felt like a completely different vehicle — and I felt different driving it. That's the part people don't expect: a clean car changes your whole day.

Friends started asking. Then their coworkers. Then strangers who saw a before-and-after photo. Somewhere between working weekends and skipping sleep, it stopped being a favor and became a business.

I named it Spotless Rides because that's the whole promise — not "pretty good," not "clean enough." Spotless. And I priced it the way I wish someone had priced it for me back when I was that kid with the faded paint: honestly, clearly, and within reach. Every price is on this page. No "call for a quote." No surprise upcharge when I show up.

Today it's still my hands, my towels, and my name on the badge. That's not a limitation — it's the point.
