Our Story

Why I started Falcore Rides

“Every owner's ride should be proud.”

— Musa Haseeb Minhas, Founder

Everybody remembers the first car they were proud of.

Maybe you saved two summers for it. Maybe it was handed down with somebody else's dent already in the door. It doesn't matter what it cost — there was a day you parked it, walked away, and turned back for one more look.

Then life gets in. The commute, the spilled coffee, the kids' cleats, the dog, the job site. Nothing dramatic happens; it just accumulates. And one day a friend asks for a ride and you're apologizing before they've got the door open — sweeping a jacket off the passenger seat, hoping they don't look down.

That's the part I actually care about. Not the dirt. The way people quietly stop seeing the car they were once proud of. You pay for it every month. You should be glad to hand somebody the keys.

I started doing this on my own car for exactly that reason. The first time I finished it properly I sat in it for ten minutes and just looked. Same car, same mileage, same everything — and I drove differently all that week. Sat up straighter. Took the long way home.

Then I did a friend's. Watching him walk a slow lap around it, grinning like he'd bought it that morning — that's when it stopped being a hobby.

I named it Falcore Rides because pride is the thing I'm really handing back; clean is just how it gets delivered. And I priced it the way I wish someone had priced it for me, back when I had a car I loved and no money to look after it. Every price is on this page. No "call for a quote." No surprise upcharge when I show up.

Still my hands, my towels, my name on the badge. Bring me the car you were proud of — you'll be proud of it again.
