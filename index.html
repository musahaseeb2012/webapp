    Things to Watch For

      Before You Buy

      • Get a pre-purchase inspection from an independent mechanic.
      • Pull a vehicle history report (Carfax/AutoCheck) using the VIN.
      • Confirm the title status matches what the seller claims.
      • Test drive it and check for warning lights, unusual noises, and fluid leaks.
      • Compare against similar listings in your area before negotiating.

      Estimates are generated from general reliability reputation and typical depreciation trends — not live market listings, VIN-decoded data, or manufacturer figures. Use this as a starting point, not a substitute for a professional inspection or a real-time valuation tool (e.g. KBB, Edmunds).
